OVERVIEW

One Platform for Policy Discovery and Understanding

Government policies and welfare schemes can be difficult to navigate when information is spread across different websites, departments, and documents.

PolicyGPT aims to make this information easier to discover and understand by bringing policy and scheme information, search, eligibility guidance, comparison, and assistance into one platform.

The platform is designed to help users explore available information, understand scheme requirements, and identify the appropriate next steps.

verified_user

Platform Transparency Note

PolicyGPT functions as an independent information intelligence and discovery platform designed to assist citizens and stakeholders in understanding public schemes. It is not an official government body, and all final determinations of eligibility and benefit disbursement reside exclusively with the designated government departments and agencies.
